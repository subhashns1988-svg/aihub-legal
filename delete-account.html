AI Hub — Delete your account

Last updated: 28 July 2026

You can delete your AI Hub account and all of its data yourself, from inside the app. This page explains how, what gets deleted, and what to do if you no longer have the app installed.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open AI Hub.
  2. Open the menu (top left) and tap Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account and all data.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, and enter your password — or re-confirm with Google if you signed in that way.
  5. Tap Delete my account.

If you signed up but never verified your email address, you are held on the "Verify your email" screen and cannot reach Settings. Delete your account from that screen instead: tap Delete this account near the bottom.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes, immediately:

  • Your AI Hub account and the email address stored with it
  • Your conversations and messages
  • Your projects, prompts, agents and knowledge snippets
  • Your saved API keys and provider connections
  • Your usage and cost history, and your profile and app preferences

There is no retention period, because there is nothing to retain: AI Hub has no server that stores your content. Your conversations and keys are held only on your device, so they are erased at the moment you confirm, and the account record is deleted from Google Firebase Authentication at the same time.

This cannot be undone.

Nothing is kept on a server, so there is no copy for us or for you to restore from. If you want to keep your chats, export them first from Settings → Backup & restore. Backup files never contain your API keys.

Your API keys with providers

Deleting your AI Hub account removes the keys stored in the app, but it does not and cannot revoke them at the provider — they are your keys, held in your OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Groq or other account. If you want them disabled entirely, revoke them from that provider's own dashboard.

If you have already uninstalled the app

Uninstalling deletes everything AI Hub stored on your device, but your account record still exists. To remove it, email getaihubapp@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we will delete the account. Reinstalling the app and using the in-app option above works too, and is immediate.
